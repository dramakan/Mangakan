Our Magical Privacy Scroll

Last Enchanted: March 2026

🌸 Welcome to the Realm!

Hello there, traveler! We are so happy you've entered the premium comic realm of Mangakan. Because we value our readers and creators deeply, protecting your personal magic (data) is super important to us! This scroll explains how we collect, use, and protect your information while you read and explore.

📜 The Information We Gather

When you wander through our manga pages or summon a new story to our library, our magical system automatically notes down a few things to make your experience better:

  • Your Magical Signature: Basic details like your IP address, browser type, and the device you are using to visit us.
  • Your Journey Paths: Which chapters you read, how long you stay, and the genres you love the most.
  • Creator Details: If you use the "Publish Manga" feature, we store the beautiful art, descriptions, and titles you share with the realm!

🍪 Magical Treats (Cookies)

Like any cozy magical cafe, we use "cookies"! These aren't the chocolate chip kind, but rather tiny digital crumbs saved on your device. They help us remember your reading progress, keep you logged in, and make the website load fast and smooth. By staying in our realm, you agree to share these little treats with us!

🧚‍♀️ Our Guild Partners (AdSense)

To keep the Mangakan realm entirely free for everyone, we partner with friendly merchants like Google AdSense. They place advertisement banners in our designated spaces.

Please note: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other places on the magical web. You can always opt out of personalized advertising by visiting your Google Ads Settings. We promise to keep the ads subtle so they don't ruin your reading vibe!

🛡️ Our Protective Barrier

We cast the strongest barrier spells we know to keep your information safe from dark wizards and data goblins. We do not sell your personal information. However, please remember that no spell is 100% unbreakable on the internet, so always share your secrets wisely!
