Pacts Forged: March 2026

📜 Agreeing to the Pacts

Greetings, traveler! By stepping into the Mangakan library, reading our scrolls, or using our "Publish Manga" spells, you are agreeing to follow the rules written in this enchanted document. If you do not agree with these rules, you must gently close this window and leave the realm.

✨ Traveler Conduct

We want this realm to be peaceful, cute, and fun for everyone. While you wander our halls, you agree NOT to:

  • Use dark magic (bots, scrapers, or scripts) to attack our servers or steal our pages.
  • Harass, bully, or cast mean spells at other readers or creators.
  • Attempt to break into the Guildmaster's restricted areas (hacking).

🎨 The Creators' Code (Copyright)

Mangakan allows users to upload their own magical stories! However, we respect the original conjurers (artists and authors) of all worlds. You may ONLY upload artwork, stories, and manga pages that you have personally created, or that you have explicit, written permission to share.

If we discover that you have stolen someone else's spellbook (copyright infringement), your uploads will be instantly banished to the void, and your access to the realm may be permanently revoked.

💎 The Merchants (Advertising)

To keep the lights glowing in our library, we allow approved merchants (like Google AdSense) to display banners. You agree not to use illusion magic (ad-blockers) in a way that maliciously harms the realm, and you agree not to artificially click on these banners to manipulate the system.

⚔️ Banishment (Termination)

The Guildmaster (Karash) holds the ultimate key to the realm. We reserve the right to remove any uploaded content, suspend accounts, or banish travelers at any time, without warning, if these sacred rules are broken.
